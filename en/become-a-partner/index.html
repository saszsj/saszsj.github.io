Become a referral partner

Know a business that needs a custom website, wants to use AI or has a task to automate? Introduce them to ZSJ and see how many of your referrals ask to speak with a consultant.

How to refer a business

  1. Apply for access

    Enter your name and email address in the application form. Once ZSJ approves your application, you’ll receive your public referral link and private dashboard code by email.

  2. Share your link

    Send only the public link to businesses you’d like to introduce. Keep the access code private so you can sign in to your dashboard.

  3. Let the business describe its project

    The business tells the chat assistant what it needs and receives a project proposal with an estimated price range. If interested, it can leave an email address for a ZSJ consultant to get in touch.

What your dashboard shows

Referred visitors
When a visitor agrees to measurement, ZSJ counts their browser once. The first recognized partner link gets credit for up to 30 days on that browser.
Contact requests
When a referred visitor asks to be contacted, their email address counts once toward your total. Your dashboard shows aggregate figures and keeps email addresses private.

Your application

Both fields are required. ZSJ’s program owner will review your application. If approved, you’ll receive your access details at the address you provide.
